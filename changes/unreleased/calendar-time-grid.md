Calendar has a phone month grid with event chips, Week and Day time grids, tap-to-create, swipe navigation, local times, and server calendar colors. Calendars can be created, renamed, recolored, deleted, and subscribed to by link, and calendars shared with you show their owner. Tasks can be ticked off from the list, and contacts can be called or emailed.
