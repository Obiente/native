Mail, News, and any other app with the same kind of data now open in shared mailbox and feed reader workspaces chosen from what the installed app's interface describes, instead of version-pinned Mail and News screens. Composing, replying, and forwarding appear when the app's own interface describes sending in a way whose result can be checked, unsent drafts are kept on the device and come back after the app restarts, and when a send's result is unclear the app checks its status where the interface allows and never repeats it without confirmation. Mail's interface does not, so Mail opens for reading and organizing without a composer, and its undo and scheduled sending, saved drafts, attachments, address suggestions, folder management, and account settings are no longer offered. Article read and star changes only ever affect the article they are made on. News opens read-only because its interface does not describe article read or star changes in a verifiable form.
