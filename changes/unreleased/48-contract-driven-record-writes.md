Record editors now follow the shape of an app's contract and data instead of its name or version, so any app with a grid of rows, records inside a parent, a questionnaire, or a recipe collection gets the same native editor. A write that the contract does not fully describe, such as several people sent as one plain string, now stays read-only with an explanation.
